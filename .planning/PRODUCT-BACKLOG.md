# Product backlog

## Near-future direction

Extend Donut's abilities through a ChatGPT plugin, so people can use their deliberately captured knowledge directly in ChatGPT through an authenticated connection to Donut.

Make deliberately captured knowledge more useful through learning and refinement. Borrow ideas from OpenKB to close the learning-to-refinement gap: use confusing recall answers and useful conversations to improve understanding and make reviewed note corrections. Improve note quality and retrieval quality so questions, search, chat, and external agents use the right evidence. Keep human authorship and learning history intact; large-scale document ingestion is not the near-future focus.
