# Product backlog

## Near-future direction

Extend Donut's abilities through a ChatGPT plugin, so people can use their deliberately captured knowledge directly in ChatGPT through an authenticated connection to Donut.

Make daily recall an opportunity to reinforce learning and improve deliberately captured notes. Start by giving connected assistants access to recent recall history through MCP so we can study actual questions, answers, and the note evidence behind misses. Use those findings to decide how to reinforce difficult material and where to reorganize or refine notes during or after recall. Borrow ideas from OpenKB to turn confusing answers and useful conversations into better understanding and reviewed note corrections. Improve retrieval so questions, search, chat, and external agents use the right evidence. Keep human authorship and learning history intact.
