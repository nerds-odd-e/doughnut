## Goal and scope

Developers can stop their isolated SUT without leaving its owned forked backend
running, so restart can reuse the allocation while a peer remains usable.
Correct the existing shutdown boundary; preserve private mock cleanup callers.
Exclude database retirement, new ownership registries, listener-based process
adoption, hard-kill recovery, and broader CLI/MCP support.
