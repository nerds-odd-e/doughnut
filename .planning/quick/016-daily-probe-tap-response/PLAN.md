# Daily probe side tap

**Status:** shipped (2026-08-30). Plan retired — measurement lives in
[daily-probe-protocol.md](../../notes/daily-probe-protocol.md).
**Type:** ad-hoc plan (`.planning/quick/`)

A learner can complete the Daily probe by tapping unlabeled left/right
zones (`pointerdown`) or with F / J / arrow keys. Same scoring, same trial
clock. Zones have no ←/→ or F/J labels. Stored trials stay `left` / `right`.
