# Spelling objectives with one skill held: retest

Review: November 17, 2026. Frozen tasks at `68ebdc1a9b`.
Baseline: [spelling objectives and cues: validation](spelling-objectives-validation-evidence.md)
(case review `0f7994a0d6`, recommendation `b1fad80d32`), left unchanged.

**Recommendation: proceed to design the paired objective, cue and rule.**
Only the paired version kept each family's declared skill answerable, and its
two readers agreed most. The design must handle what this retest left open:
a several-answer format for brine, an unforced noun for 伝手, the ～かかる
alternative, and meadow overlap. This recommendation authorizes no
implementation, paid calls or learner recruitment.

## Provenance and method

Inputs are the archived cues of the five baseline families, from the November
13 private export (SHA-256
`3071dac175df47cd172c6bbc54f8fe887f475658f950ca340e9e441dde0ad3df`, rechecked).
For each family one skill was declared and held, then three versions were
frozen before any reader saw them:

- **C1 manual note/alias review:** archived cue after an author's repair.
  Only meadow's note text changed ("often mown for hay" replaces the grazing
  wording); other families changed aliases only, so C1 and C3 share four cue texts.
- **C2 paired objective/cue/rule:** new cue, declared objective and an
  answer-neutral rule (script, format and blank instructions, no answer list).
- **C3 broader acceptance:** archived cue unchanged; a broad judge rule.

Six fresh AI reader contexts, two per condition, each saw one condition's
version of every family and nothing else, with no web access. Phase 1: answer,
the task they inferred, and what they had to retrieve; frozen. Phase 2: the
scoring rule without a key and a shuffled response pool (learner answers mixed
with controls and added variants), marked A accept, R reject, U unresolved.
C1 judges were not shown the alias list, since it is the key; they judged as a
careful author would. Provisional marks frozen before reading are compared below;
like the baseline's, they are informed proposals, not ground truth.

Independence limits: readers are the same AI model family; the pool itself
revealed likely answers (see 伝手); all judgments are AI task judgments.

## Per family

Marks are shown as reader a/reader b, then the provisional mark in brackets.

### brine — held: word-family production (noun, gerund, participle)

Archived slots mask Brining, brine and brined; the product takes one word.
The baseline's revised cue kept only the noun and dropped the process and
inflection demand. C2 numbered the six slots and asked for each form.

- Phase 1: C1 answered brine and brining; C3 brine and brining; both flagged
  that the masks hide several forms, so the expected word is unclear. C2 readers
  produced all six forms correctly (case aside). C2b noted the shortcut: once
  one slot is solved, the rest follow from "same word".
- Leakage: visible `[[curing]]` link (archived) in every condition; Wikipedia
  text recognised by C2a and C3a.
- C2 judges matched the provisional marks exactly: only the correct six-form
  answer A; single words, all-brine and a list ending in curing R.
- C1: brining A/A; brine A/U and brined A/U [A]; six-form answers R/R [U].
- C3: correct six-form list R/A [A]; C3a misread slot 6 as visible curing and
  so also gave the curing list U [R]. brined U/R [U]; brine A/U [A].
- Genuine form error kept distinct: brime R in all six judgments; curing
  (a different word) R throughout.

### 伝手 — held: whole-expression production (noun + particle + verb)

The baseline's revision asked only for the reading of 伝手, which changes the
skill to reading. C2 described the meaning without コネ and asked for
noun・particle・dictionary-form verb, no loanwords.

- Phase 1: all four C1 and C3 readers answered 他力本願, with low confidence.
  Under the original cue the whole expression is not answerable. C2 readers
  answered つてを頼る and 伝手を頼る, and listed other natural answers
  (人脈を頼る, 縁故を頼る, つてをたどる): the cue cannot force 伝手 without
  supplying it.
- Leakage: コネ in the C1/C3 cue. The response pool then revealed the answer:
  C1a ("the responses suggest the title is 伝手を頼る") and C3b changed their
  view in phase 2.
- C2: を forms A/A [A]; 人脈を頼る A/U [U]; コネを使う R/R; つて R/R.
- C1: 伝手を頼る A/U [A]; つてをたよる and つてを頼る U/U [A]: readers could
  not tell which scripts an author would list. コネを使う R/U and
  人脈を頼る R/U [R]; つて U/U [R].
- C3: を forms A/A [A]; コネを使う U/U [A]; 人脈を頼る U/U [A]; つて U/U [R].
- Genuine errors: でんてをたよる (wrong reading) and 自力で行う R everywhere.
- **伝手に頼る: unresolved.** U in all six judgments. A web source check
  (November 17, 2026) found only を forms in Oggi (https://oggi.jp/7404266)
  and デジタル大辞泉 via Weblio (https://www.weblio.jp/content/つて, 「―を頼って
  就職する」); a 2014 blog (https://nihongomemo.hatenablog.jp/entry/2014/08/15/071151)
  calls 伝手を頼る the typical form without calling に wrong. Kotobank
  returned 404; BCCWJ (少納言), NINJAL-LWP and yourei.jp were not usable.
  Settling it needs a BCCWJ count or 明鏡/日本国語大辞典.

### おっしゃる — held: honorific lexical written form

Archived title おっしゃる, alias 仰る; the note says either script is fine.
C2 used a present-tense dictionary-form blank and dropped the archived
inflected example slots.

- Phase 1: all six answered おっしゃる; four also named 仰る. C1/C3 readers
  said the partial masks (stem おっしゃ) and the double-honorific warning nearly
  give the answer; C2b said the instruction names the category.
- Script variants: 仰る and おっしゃる A in all six judgments, applying the
  note's script permission. Kept separate from usage questions.
- Genuine error: おしゃる (missing っ) R in all six.
- C3: おっしゃった R/R and 言われる R/R [both U]: readers were stricter than
  the provisional marks. 申す and おっしゃられる R everywhere.

### ～かける — held: construction retrieval and formation

The baseline's revision supplied the suffix; C2 kept two blanks before た and
ている, did not name かける, but described the construction category.

- Phase 1: all six answered 死にかけ. C2 readers said the instruction naming
  "verb + auxiliary meaning just before a state" and the Chinese gloss (死)
  supplied much; the remaining retrieval was the auxiliary and the stem.
- 死にかかっ: C1 U/R [R]; C2 A/A [U]; C3 A/A [U]. Readers credit ～かかる as the
  same meaning; whether the declared skill means かける only is unresolved.
- Script variants: C2 and C3 accepted 死に掛け and しにかけ; C1 judges left them
  U/U [A], not knowing what an author lists.
- Genuine errors: 死ぬかけ (connecting form) R everywhere; 死にかけた
  (duplicated た) R everywhere, including C3 [U]; 死にそう(だ) R everywhere.

### meadow — held: vocabulary retrieval (ambiguity control)

- Phase 1: all six answered meadow; each flagged grassland, pasture or
  prairie as near alternatives.
- C1 (repaired note, "mown for hay"): pasture and grassland R/R [R]; the
  repair moved them from overlap to wrong for these readers.
- C2 and C3: pasture U/U, grassland U/U [U]. This overlap is ambiguity, not
  error. field R/R in C2 and C3 [U].
- Genuine error: medow R in all six; forest R.

## Comparison

| Aspect | C1 manual note/alias | C2 paired objective/cue/rule | C3 broader acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skill held | brine: one word passes, family not tested; 伝手: not answerable (他力本願 ×2) | all five answerable; brine needs six answers; 伝手 noun not forced | same as C1 cue: brine unclear, 伝手 not answerable |
| Legitimate variants | readers unsure which scripts an author lists (U) | script variants credited; ～かかる and 人脈を頼る credited by some | variants credited, plus paraphrases U |
| Genuine errors | rejected | rejected | rejected |
| Ambiguity | hidden by note repair (meadow) | kept visible (meadow, 伝手, かかる) | kept visible, more U on paraphrases |
| Reader disagreement | most: brine, 伝手, かかる | least: one response (人脈を頼る) | brine (one misreading), brine/brined |
| Effort | author repair time unmeasured | cue, rule and new format per item | none per item; does not specify the task |

Both alternatives leave the archived task unclear: the manual repair cannot
make 伝手 answerable without rewriting the cue, and broader acceptance credits
answers to a question readers could not infer.

## Effort

- Preparation (slice 1): 3.9 active minutes (agent tool-call span; thinking
  time between calls excluded, so a lower bound).
- Review orchestration: about 10 active minutes, excluding reader wait
  (approximate). Reader wall time 19–29 s per answer pass, 20–87 s per judge
  pass; about 42–49k tokens per reader context.
- Human author time for any condition: unavailable.

## Limits

- Five families, two AI readers per condition; no human readers, no deployed
  grading, no usability or learning-gain measurement.
- Readers judged sentence fit, not the product's one-typed-answer check;
  brine's C2 six-answer format does not exist in the product.
- C1 judges estimated alias lists; their U marks measure that uncertainty.
- Phase 2 pools leaked likely answers (伝手).
- 伝手に頼る and ～かかる remain open language questions.

## Follow-up count and decisions (November 17, 2026)

A count over the same export (2,602 spelling answers, 784 notes, 402 wrong)
checked how often the two failing shapes cost the learner an answer.

- **Several hidden forms (brine).** No wrong answer was the expected English
  word with -s, -ed or -ing. The prompt hides those forms on purpose so they
  do not reveal the title.
- **Whole expressions (伝手を頼る).** About 36 of 784 notes; 24 wrong of 117
  answers (21%) against 15% for one-word notes. About five of those misses
  were a variant of the expected expression (draw a line, got a hold of,
  伝手に頼る, つてをたよる, 目にあまる).
- **Same word in another script or form.** About 15 of the 118 wrong answers
  that were neither blank nor another note.
- **Decisions.** The owner accepted the recommendation to drop a
  several-answer spelling format and a warning for notes that fit spelling
  poorly. Variants are handled by [accepting a wrong spelling answer as an alias](SEED-073-learning-refinement-and-retrieval.md#accept-spelling-answer-as-alias).
  The paired objective, cue and rule design is not queued.
- **Largest group.** 227 of the 402 wrong answers named another of the
  learner's notes; the detail is with [short lessons after recall misses](learning-refinement-validation-approaches.md#validate-short-recall-lessons).
- **Limits.** Note text is exported only for wrong answers, so hidden forms
  could be rebuilt for 82 notes. The 118 other wrong answers were sorted by
  reading, not by a rule. A cue hint that names another answer (コネ) could
  not be counted.
