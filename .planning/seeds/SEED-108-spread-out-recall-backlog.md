# Spread out my recall backlog in one click

<a id="spread-out-recall-backlog"></a>

### Spread out my recall backlog in one click

**Identity:** SEED-108#spread-out-recall-backlog
```json dough-story-state
{"schemaVersion":1,"refinement":"not-refined","approach":"unselected"}
```

**Beneficiary** A Donut learner returning after a busy period to hundreds of due recall items.

**Goal** Resume daily recall with a manageable workload today and a clear view of what is due over the next 30 days.

**Scope** One click in User Settings applies the redistribution of currently due items and shows the resulting forecast. Use today and the next 30 local calendar days as the scheduling window. Count items already scheduled for each day and fill lighter days from the backlog so today's remaining workload is close to the average for the next 30 days. Give earlier slots to items whose recall is expected to suffer more from waiting, such as recently learned or less stable memories. Keep the dates of items already scheduled for future days.

**Forecast** Show today's remaining count, the average for the next 30 days, and the scheduled count for each of those days. Counts reflect the schedule at the time of redistribution and can change as recalls generate follow-ups.

**Example** A learner has 520 items due today and 100 already scheduled over the next 30 days. If no future day already exceeds 20, a single click leaves 20 due today and shows 20 scheduled on each of the next 30 days.
